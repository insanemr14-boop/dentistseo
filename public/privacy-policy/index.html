Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 August 2026

Dentist SEO Service ("we", "us") is operated by RioCloud Solutions, Chandigarh, India. This policy explains what we collect when you use dentistseo.dpdns.org, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We have tried to write it in plain English rather than legal boilerplate.

What we collect

There are only two ways we receive information from you.

1. Information you type into a form. Our contact form asks for your name, email address, phone number and your message. We use these solely to reply to your enquiry and, if you become a client, to deliver the service you asked for. Submissions are emailed to info@riocloudsolutions.com. We do not sell this information, rent it, or pass it to anyone outside our own team.

2. Information collected automatically. Like almost every website, our servers and analytics tools record technical details of your visit: pages viewed, approximate location derived from your IP address, referring site, browser and device type, and time spent on the page. This is aggregate traffic data used to understand what content is useful. It is not used to identify you personally.

Cookies and similar technologies

We use three categories of cookies:

  • Strictly necessary — required for the site to function and for remembering your cookie choice. These are always on and cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics — Google Analytics 4, which tells us how many people visit and which articles they read. IP addresses are anonymised.
  • Advertising — Google AdSense, described in the next section.

When you first arrive, a banner asks whether you consent to analytics and advertising cookies. Until you accept, those categories stay switched off. You can change your mind at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings, which makes the banner appear again on your next visit.

Advertising and Google AdSense

This site is free to read and is funded partly by advertising. We use Google AdSense to serve those ads.

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on this site.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of third-party vendor cookies more broadly at aboutads.info/choices or youronlinechoices.com.

Third-party advertising networks other than Google may also be used from time to time. Where that happens, those vendors set their own cookies and are governed by their own privacy policies, which we do not control.

How long we keep things

Contact enquiries are retained for up to 24 months so we can refer back to prior conversations, then deleted. Analytics data is retained for 14 months, the Google Analytics default. Cookie preferences live in your own browser and are never sent to us.

Your rights

You can ask us what personal data we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted entirely. Email info@riocloudsolutions.com and we will respond within 30 days. If you are in the EEA or UK, you also have the right to object to processing and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Children

This site offers marketing services to dental practices and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will remove it.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS and form submissions are transmitted over an encrypted connection. No method of transmission over the internet is perfectly secure, so while we take reasonable measures we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially we will update the "last updated" date at the top of the page. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the revised policy.
